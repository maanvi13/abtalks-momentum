# ABTalks Momentum

## Vision

ABTalks Momentum is a mobile-first redesign of the ABTalks 60-Day Coding Challenge.

Instead of focusing on maintaining a perfect streak, the experience encourages students to build sustainable momentum.

Students often use the platform late at night after college when they are mentally exhausted.

The redesign focuses on motivation, consistency, reflection, and long-term growth rather than pressure.

---

# Problem

Students don't stop coding because they lack ability.

They stop because motivation slowly disappears.

Current productivity apps reward perfection.

Our redesign rewards progress.

---

# Design Philosophy

Build Momentum, Not Pressure.

Every screen should answer one question:

"How do we motivate students to return tomorrow?"

---

# Target Users

College students participating in the 60-day coding challenge.

Most users

- use mobile phones
- use the platform at night
- are beginners
- want internships
- want stronger GitHub profiles
- want consistency

---

# Goals

• Encourage consistency

• Reduce guilt after missed days

• Make progress feel visible

• Connect daily work with career growth

• Keep the experience emotionally encouraging
